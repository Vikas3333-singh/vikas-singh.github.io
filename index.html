A short introduction

I support the people who can’t afford a bad IT day: 15+ Senior Partners and Managing Directors at Boston Consulting Group, and the 300+ colleagues around them. When something repeats, I write a tool so it stops.

Eleven years across desks, devices and identity: resolving incidents, standing up laptops with Intune and Autopilot, running joiner-mover-leaver access in Entra ID, and keeping boardroom AV ready before anyone walks in.

My PowerShell and Batch troubleshooting utility went into BCG’s standard laptop image across 250+ machines. Ticket Forge, a Python and Selenium bot, files Lenovo warranty requests so nobody has to retype them.
