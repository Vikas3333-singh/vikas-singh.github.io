A Little About Me

When technology issues interrupt high-stakes business, end users need more than just a ticket number. They need clear communication, calm diagnosis, and a fix that prevents the problem from recurring.

Throughout my 11+ years in enterprise IT workplace technology, I have specialized in bridging high-touch executive support with scalable infrastructure engineering. At Boston Consulting Group (BCG), I served as the primary deskside escalation engineer for 300+ users and the dedicated point of contact for 15+ Senior Partners and Managing Directors—consistently sustaining 95%+ SLA compliance.

I believe in a true shift-left operational model: identifying repeating support pain-points and resolving them through automation. My self-developed 8-in-1 Windows troubleshooting utility was pitched to IT leadership and formally integrated into BCG's corporate master OS image across 250+ laptops. Additionally, my Python/Selenium tool (Ticket Forge) automated 30+ monthly Lenovo warranty service requests, eliminating 10+ hours of manual administrative overhead.
